# Filesystem boundary
> What kb reads, copies, edits and deletes on disk: entry discovery, symlinks, path arguments, and (from 1.5) in-place sources across a project.

- **Category:** external boundary · data integrity
- **Status:** needs work
- **Where:** `internal/entry` (`Discover`, `Resolve`), `internal/cli/add.go` (copy and rollback), `rm.go` (directory deletes), `edit.go` (`$EDITOR`); `internal/config` `Read`/`ReadLocation` via `readCapped` (store `config.toml` and `.kb/kb.toml`: regular files only, 1 MiB cap enforced by stat and by a limited read) and `cli.loadConfig`; `internal/workspace` (`ResolveLocation`, `TargetLocation`, `Location.Store`, `ValidName`: location discovery, `--root`, `--global`, store names), `cli/init.go` (the only creator of `.kb/`); planned configured globs (1.5). `internal/links` (5.1) resolves paths purely, with no disk access: it is not part of this boundary until 5.2 matches link targets to entries.
- **Why it matters:** a mistake here deletes or exposes user files outside the store. In-place sources widen it from `data/` to whole repositories.
- **Evidence:** the harness found that symlinks under `data/` pointing outside the root are followed and indexed (DEFAULTS #65, carried to 1.5). `path-traversal` scenarios pass.
