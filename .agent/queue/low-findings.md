# Low-findings queue

Small, mechanical fixes found by the standards passes (or noticed while working) that aren't
worth stopping an item for. The orchestrator drains this queue with **Haiku** sub-agents. Rules:
AGENTS.md "Low-findings queue".

Entry format, one per finding, newest at the bottom of **Open**:

```
- [ ] Q<n> · <file:line or package> · <what's wrong> → <suggested fix> · from: <wave/pass or item> · <YYYY-MM-DD>
```

- Claimed: change `[ ]` to `[~]` and append `· claimed: <state-log name>`.
- Too big for a quick fix: change to `[!]`, append `· needs: sonnet|opus — <why>`, and leave it.
  The orchestrator moves it to a PLAN item or a Sonnet fix-up.
- Done: move the line to **Done** as `[x]`, with the test command that passed. The orchestrator
  prunes Done after each wave.
