# Harness-improvements queue

Suggested improvements to the adversarial tools (`.agent/scripts/adversarial/`, the
`adversarial-testing` skill, `docs/ADVERSARIAL_TESTING.md`) that came out of wave-end passes or
daily work. Not urgent: pick them up when a wave has spare capacity, or when a later wave
touches the area. Rules: AGENTS.md "Harness-improvements queue".

Entry format, newest at the bottom of **Open**:

```
- [ ] H<n> · <tool or skill> · <gap: what the wave changed that no harness/mutation operator probes> → <suggested improvement> · value: high|med|low · from: <wave> · <YYYY-MM-DD>
```

- Claimed: change `[ ]` to `[~]` and append `· claimed: <state-log name>`.
- Done: move the line to **Done** as `[x]`, with how the improvement was validated (e.g. "new
  scenario fails on the old binary, passes now", "operator kills N more mutants").
- Rejected: move it to **Done** as `[-]` with the reason.
